About Me

I am an International Relations and European Studies graduate with hands-on experience at UN Trade and Development (UNCTAD) in Geneva. My work sits at the intersection of policy, data, and human communication.

I don't just analyze data; I communicate it. Whether I am translating complex UN Global Survey findings into visual reports, designing multilingual e-learning modules for WTO Trade Facilitation, or supporting digital transformation for national trade portals, my goal is always the same: making international development clear, accessible, and actionable.

With a background spanning cultural mediation, academic research, and international capacity building, I bring a nuanced, cross-cultural perspective to every project. I am comfortable working across cultures, managing stakeholder communications, and leveraging tools like R, Power BI, and Python to create better storytelling and support decision-making.

Core Focus

  • ◆ Communicating complex trade and policy issues to diverse audiences.
  • ◆ Designing and administering multilingual e-learning and capacity-building programs.
  • ◆ Using data visualization (R, Power BI) to support the narrative.
